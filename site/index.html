PLAY

Score like an arcade.

Faster play means bigger multipliers. Connect clues and form the loop before the multiplier clock winds down. Complete with zero invalid branches to lock in a perfect run and climb the highscore tiers.

Time multiplier

Your score factor starts high and slowly ticks down. Laying down loop segments swiftly rewards you with top tier points.

hurry up for more points

Single loop

Form one unbroken, non-intersecting loop around numerical clues. Each cell clue tells you exactly how many sides are part of the loop.

one loop · no branches

Quick crosses

Mark crosses to rule out empty edges. Clicking a satisfied or zero clue instantly crosses out all free sides for swift solving.

auto-cross satisfied clues

Autosave & resume

Close the window whenever. Your puzzle state, lines, crosses, and timer are automatically saved and ready to resume.

pick up where you left off

INSTALL

On your Omarchy desktop.

From the project folder, run the installer, then launch the game. It fits your Omarchy look and works fully offline — no accounts, no network.

Terminal Install from the project folder
$ ./install.sh

  1. Run the installer. ./install.sh sets up the launcher and desktop entry.
  2. Launch the game. Run omarchy-slitherlink, or find Slitherlink in the launcher.
  3. Play offline. It follows your Omarchy look and works fully offline.
